About me

EARNING BELIEF BEFORE ADOPTION

My work starts wherever something new needs to earn belief before it earns adoption. That might be an AI product enterprise buyers don't fully trust yet, a family tea brand entering online retail for the first time, an event format executives haven't experienced before, or technical standards a business audience finds unfamiliar.

3 years in, the job hasn't changed: translate the unfamiliar into something clear enough, and credible enough, for people to say yes.

How I work

FOUR STEPS, EVERY CAMPAIGN

01

Find what is holding people back

I start with the audience, the business objective, and the point where people lose interest, confidence, or motivation to act.

02

Build the story around that

What needs to be understood first, what needs to be believed, and what evidence makes the next step feel easier.

03

Connect the touchpoints

Content, social, events, PR, landing pages, e-commerce, and follow-up systems all supporting the same campaign idea.

04

Measure what moves

I track what happened, find where the journey can improve, and refine on evidence rather than assumptions.
